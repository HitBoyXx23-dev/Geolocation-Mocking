💻 DevTools Geolocation Mocking

  • Press Shift + Ctrl + I (or Cmd + Option + I on Mac) to open Developer Tools.
  • Press Shift + Ctrl + P to open the Command Menu.
  • Type sensors and hit Enter to open the Sensors tab.
  • Scroll to “Geolocation” and pick a preset or custom location.

📜 Console Mocking Code:


navigator.geolocation.getCurrentPosition = function(success) {
  success({
    coords: {
      latitude: 37.7749,
      longitude: -122.4194,
      accuracy: 10
    }
  });
};
console.log("✅ Geolocation overridden to San Francisco");
